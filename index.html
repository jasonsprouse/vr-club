WELCOME TO PARADIGM XR CLUB

An immersive VR/XR audio-reactive nightclub featuring music and shorts from Good Faith Paradigm (@GparadigmOrgLove).

WebXR VR Mode

  • Enter VR: Click the ENTER VR button below.
  • Locomotion: Left thumbstick to walk, right thumbstick to snap-turn.
  • Teleport: Pull trigger or squeeze grip towards floor.
  • DJ Booth: Aim laser pointer at 3D console buttons to DJ!

Desktop Controls

  • Look: Click anywhere on screen to lock mouse.
  • Walk: W A S D or Arrow keys.
  • Sprint: Hold Shift.
  • Play / Pause: Press Space or click 3D DJ console.
  • Unlock Mouse: Press Esc.
